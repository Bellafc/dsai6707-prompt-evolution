Method. For each item I ran a web search, then read the primary page. For ACL Anthology papers that meant the paper page and its `.bib` export; for NeurIPS and ICLR, the proceedings.neurips.cc / proceedings.iclr.cc paper page and its BibTeX export. For ICML 2026 it was the PMLR page, and for Anthropic's circuit-tracing paper the transformer-circuits.pub page itself. Where there was a DOI I also read the publisher's Crossref deposit. Every arXiv id comes from the arXiv API record (authors in order, versions, comments, journal reference). For ICLR, the decision and OpenReview id come from the ICLR virtual-site paper listing (`iclr.cc/static/virtual/data/iclr-<year>-orals-posters.json`), because openreview.net returns a bot challenge. To check whether a paper has a peer-reviewed version, I downloaded these official indexes and searched them by title: NeurIPS 2021-2025, ICLR 2024-2026 proceedings, PMLR v235/v267/v306 (ICML 2024/2025/2026) and the TMLR paper list. No author, page, DOI or arXiv id was taken from memory.

Notes column. **Abstract:** gives one sentence copied verbatim from the abstract of the cited version (the published one where it exists). Only typography was normalised: straight quotes, and line-break spacing restored. Where the thesis cites a paper for something that is in the body rather than the abstract, the notes give the section where I found it in the PDF.

## Wording checks against the current draft (ch2_literature_review.md, Section 2.5.4)

The draft's claims match the sources, with four exceptions:
- lightman2024lets: the draft says step-level reward models "supervise reasoning better than labels of the final answer alone". The abstract limits this to MATH ("for training models to solve problems from the challenging MATH dataset"); consider adding "on competition mathematics".
- atanasova2023faithfulness: the draft says the tests insert or edit "the factors an explanation names". In the paper, the counterfactual editor inserts new reasons that flip the prediction and checks whether the explanation mentions them. The second test rebuilds the input from the reasons the explanation states and checks whether the prediction stays the same.
- parcalabescu2024measuring: "which only access to the model can establish" goes beyond the abstract. The abstract says CC-SHAP brings the measure "closer to the internals of the model than strictly surface output-oriented tests".
- ameisen2025circuit: the attribution graphs describe a replacement model (cross-layer transcoders) trained to approximate the model, checked by interventions. A sentence saying so would protect the claim that the mechanisms are "inside the model".
