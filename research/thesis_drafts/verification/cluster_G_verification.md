## Facts across entries relevant to the thesis design (read from the papers)
- Excluding tool or retrieval output from the training loss is documented in: AgentTuning Sec. 2.2.3 (loss only on the model's output), ToRA Eq. 4 (loss on rationales and actions given tool outputs), FireAct App. A.7 (with vs without observation masking; no consistent effect), and Search-R1 (retrieved-token masking for stable RL, stated in the abstract).
- Where the training methods are named: RLVR in Tulu 3 (abstract: "a novel method we call"), GRPO in DeepSeekMath (abstract: "we introduce"), Rejection sampling Fine-Tuning (RFT) in Yuan et al. 2023 (abstract: "we propose to apply"). Sample, filter on binary feedback, fine-tune, repeat: ReST-EM (abstract). Retry wrong answers with the correct answer given, then fine-tune on rationales that reach correct answers: STaR (abstract).
