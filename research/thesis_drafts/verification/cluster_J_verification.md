No author, page, DOI or arXiv id in the .bib was taken from memory. A field that could not be read from such a source was left out and the entry marked PARTIAL.

Notes column. Text in quotes is exact; for Snodgrass and Ahn (1985) the only readable copies are OCR scans, and only obvious OCR letter errors were corrected (said in the row). Anything after the quoted sentence is a separate remark, not part of the abstract.
