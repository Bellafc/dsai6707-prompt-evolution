 Fuzzy Inclusion and equality
 A  B (A is a subset of B) if A(x)  B(x), for every x  X.

 A = B (A is equal to B) if A(x) = B(x), for every x  X.
 A  B (A and B are not equal) if A(x)  B(x), for at least one x  X.
