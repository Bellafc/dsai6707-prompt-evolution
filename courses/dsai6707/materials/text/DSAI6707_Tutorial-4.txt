By considering the individual and combined effect of reproduction, crossover and mutation in
genetic algorithms, derive the schemata theorem to examine the growth rates of various schemata
or similarity templates contained in a population. The schemata theorem is given as,
                                       fit ( S )            d (S )             
             n s (t + 1)  n s (t )             1 − Pcross        − Pm o( S ) 
                                       fit                l −1               
A population contains the following strings and fitness values at generation 0:
      #       String Fitness
      1       10001 20
      2       11100 10
      3       00011 5
      4       01110 15
The probability of mutation is Pm = 0.01 and the probability of crossover is Pcross = 1. Using the
schemata theorem to estimate the expected number of schemata of the form 1**** in generation 1.
