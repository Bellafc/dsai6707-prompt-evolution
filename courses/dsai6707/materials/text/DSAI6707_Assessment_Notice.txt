   DSAI6707 Assessment Details and Important Dates

Dear students,


Thank you for taking DSAI6707. Now that we are more than halfway through the course, we would
like to formally announce the assessment details.



1 Research Project 40%
The requirements for the project, “Evolutionary Computation for Emerging AI,” have already been
posted in the Files section on Canvas. Please email all required submission materials to Yajie
Zhang (23036271R@connect.polyu.hk) by 2 November.
Project presentations will take place on 4, 11, and 18 November. Each student will have six minutes
to introduce their project. Individual presentation slots are listed in Fig. 1.



2 Assignment 20%
An assignment PDF has been uploaded to Canvas. It contains two multiple-choice questions and
two calculation questions. Please complete the assignment after class and email your answers in
PDF format to Yinsong Yan (23038884R@connect.polyu.hk) by 23 November.
This assignment also serves as a set of sample questions to help you become familiar with the
question formats for the final examination.



3 Final Examination 40%
To give you sufficient time to revise, the final examination is scheduled as follows:
• Date: 25 November
• Time: 08:30–11:30
• Venue: HHB201, our usual classroom
The examination will carry 100 marks and consist of:
• 10 multiple-choice questions: 3 marks each, totalling 30 marks.
• 6 longer questions: worth 10, 10, 10, 10, 10, and 20 marks, respectively, totalling 70 marks.
You may bring a calculator and one A4 sheet of notes. You may decide what content to include on
the sheet.


Please take note of these deadlines and arrangements, and plan your work and revision accordingly.
